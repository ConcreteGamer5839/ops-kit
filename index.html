Questions

Is this for beginners?

Yes, if you've finished a manuscript. The guide explains each step from scratch. Experienced authors usually get the most from the EPUB validation chapter, the royalty spreadsheet and the checklists.

Do I need Excel?

No. The spreadsheets work in Excel, Google Sheets, Apple Numbers and LibreOffice.

Is it up to date?

Rules were checked against KDP Help on 1 October 2026, including the July 2026 eBook pricing change and the June 2025 paperback royalty change. Appendix A lists every source.

Does it cover paperbacks and hardcovers?

Paperbacks in full. Hardcover follows the same process with different cover and printing-cost tables, which the guide points to.

Does it cover AI-written books?

It covers KDP's AI-disclosure rules so you answer those questions correctly. It isn't a guide to producing books with AI.
