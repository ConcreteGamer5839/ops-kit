Getting started

  1. A Confluence admin installs the app from the Atlassian Marketplace.
  2. Any user opens Apps → Page Hygiene Report in the Confluence top navigation.
  3. Pick a staleness threshold and the spaces to check, then click Run report.
  4. Use the tabs (Needs attention, Ownership problems, Stale, All pages), the space bars, and the filter box to narrow results.
  5. Click Export CSV to download exactly what is on screen.

FAQ

What counts as stale? A page whose latest version is older than the threshold you choose (30–730 days).

What does "Owner deactivated" mean? The page owner's Atlassian account status is inactive or closed — usually someone who left the company. Reassign the page owner or archive the page.

What does "Owner not found" mean? The page lists an owner whose account Confluence could not return. Treat it like a missing owner.

Why don't I see some spaces or pages? The app runs with your own permissions. If you can't open a page in Confluence, the app won't show it. Admins see the most.

Does the app change anything? No. It is read-only. It never edits, moves, archives, or deletes pages.

Does it store our data? No. Results live only in your browser tab. Nothing is saved on our side or in Forge storage.

How big a site can it handle? It reads 250 pages per request and shows progress as it goes. A single run stops at 25,000 pages; select fewer spaces to cover the rest.

The app says my license is not active. A Confluence admin can renew or start a trial in Settings → Apps → Manage apps. Without a license you can preview five pages from one space.

I see "Confluence is rate limiting requests". Wait a minute and run the report again, or select fewer spaces.
