Privacy Policy — Page Hygiene Report for Confluence

Effective date: 1 October 2026 Provider: Wishbone Data ("we", "us"), an individual seller operating as Walter Washburn Contact: walter@chickenandairlinemiles.com

This policy explains how the Page Hygiene Report for Confluence app (the "App") handles data. The short version: the App does not collect, store, or transmit your data anywhere outside Atlassian.

1. What the App does

The App reads page and user information from your Confluence Cloud site and displays a report in your browser. It is built on Atlassian Forge and runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure.

2. Data the App reads

When a user opens the App and runs a report, the App reads, through Atlassian's Confluence REST API and with that user's own permissions:

  • Space ID, key, name and type
  • Page ID, title, space, owner account ID, creator account ID, last-editor account ID, created and last-modified dates, version number, page link
  • For owner and last-editor accounts: display name and account status (active, inactive, closed, unknown)

The App does not read page body content, comments, attachments, or email addresses.

3. Data the App stores

None. The App has no database and does not use Forge storage. Report results exist only in the memory of the user's browser tab and are discarded when the tab is closed or refreshed. CSV exports are generated in the browser and saved directly to the user's device; we never receive them.

4. Data the App transmits

None outside Atlassian. The App declares no external network access (egress), no remote back ends, and no third-party analytics. All processing happens in Atlassian-hosted Forge functions and in the user's browser. The App is designed to qualify for Atlassian's "Runs on Atlassian" program.

5. Logs

Forge keeps function logs in Atlassian's infrastructure. The App only logs error status codes and error messages; it does not log page titles, user names, account IDs, or report contents. We may view these logs through the Atlassian developer console to diagnose errors.

6. Data we receive from Atlassian as a Marketplace partner

Through the Atlassian Marketplace we may receive licensing and billing contact information about the customer organization (for example, company name, technical/billing contact name and email, site URL, license tier). We use it only to provide support, manage licenses, and meet legal and tax obligations. This information is handled under the Atlassian Marketplace Partner Agreement.

7. Support communications

If you email us for support, we receive whatever you choose to send (name, email address, message, screenshots). We use it only to answer your request and keep it no longer than needed for that purpose and our legal obligations (typically up to 24 months). Please do not send us page content or personal data that isn't needed to fix the problem.

8. Data residency

Because the App stores no customer data, there is no app-held data to relocate. Processing occurs in Atlassian's Forge platform, which follows the data residency of your Atlassian site.

9. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and similar laws)

The App does not hold personal data about your users, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete from the App. For support emails or Marketplace contact data we hold, you may request access, correction or deletion at walter@chickenandairlinemiles.com. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

10. Security

The App requests read-only permissions, runs every request as the viewing user (so it cannot reveal pages that user cannot already see), and validates all inputs. Report a security issue to walter@chickenandairlinemiles.com with subject "SECURITY".

11. Children

The App is a business tool and is not directed to children.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above. A change that would allow the App to store or transmit customer data would be announced on the Marketplace listing before it takes effect.
